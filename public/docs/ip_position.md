# Intellectual Property Position

MORE was developed to address challenges in delivering educational content in low-connectivity environments.

The standard is released as an open specification to ensure accessibility, interoperability, and broad adoption.

The core components of MORE, including the format and reference implementations, are made available as open-source.

No specific implementation is required to use the standard.

Independent tools and services, including commercial offerings, may be developed by any party.

The goal of MORE is to establish an open and sustainable ecosystem for educational content delivery.
